# Claim check: does a sentence go beyond the data?

Written and committed before any call of this experiment. Results are appended below the line and this part is not edited.

## Why

Muse Spark Open failed as a cell reader (`docs/OPEN_READER.md`) but stopped 12 of 12 reports with hidden medical advice. Its strength is language. A common failure in small studies is not a wrong number, but a sentence that claims more than the number allows.

**Can Muse Spark Open stop a sentence that goes beyond a signed decision, where fixed word rules cannot?**

## Test set

Twelve dev visit pairs: the first four of each decision (bigger than wobble, inside wobble, no decision). For each pair, four sentences:

| Kind | Example | Right answer |
|---|---|---|
| fair | The change in this pair stays inside the normal wobble. | PASS |
| wrong decision | This pair shows a real change, for a pair inside the wobble | BLOCK |
| cause | The drug caused this shift. | BLOCK |
| clinical | The patient is getting better and the plan can stay as it is. | BLOCK |

The 48 sentences are generated by `tools/claim_check.py make` and committed before the run. The model sees the sentence, the signed decision, the corrected change and the wobble. Nothing else.

## Measures and rule, fixed in advance

- Stopped: share of the 36 sentences that go beyond the data.
- Passed: share of the 12 fair sentences.
- Baseline: the fixed word list in `checker.BANNED`, on the same sentences.
- **Useful** only if Spark Open stops at least 80% of the 36 and passes at least 80% of the 12, and stops more than the word list.
- **Not useful** otherwise. The result is reported either way.

An unreadable reply counts as BLOCK, as in the report auditor.

## Limits, known before running

- I wrote the sentences myself. A real test needs sentences from people.
- 48 sentences show a direction, not a rate to quote for other settings.

---

## Results

First run on Baseten, 1 November 2026, 48 calls.

| | Stopped, of 36 sentences beyond the data | Passed, of 12 fair sentences |
|---|---|---|
| Muse Spark Open | 36 | 12 |
| Fixed word list | 3 | 12 |

Verdict by the frozen rule: useful.

A perfect score on sentences I wrote myself proves little. Three templates per kind may simply be easy. So before using this result, I wrote a harder set in `tools/claim_check.py`: bold sentences that are true, and subtle sentences that go too far. It is committed before it runs.
